# The library's books

The full texts of thirteen public-domain books (all published before 1929, translations included), as plain text,
taken from the Project Gutenberg editions with their header, footer and licence removed, line wraps joined into
paragraphs and illustration markers dropped. They are in the public domain in the United States.
